ABOUT THE STUDIO

Small studio. Weird ideas. Games that glitch on purpose.

// THE STUDIO

Paradox EXE Games is an independent game development studio dedicated to creating fun, immersive, and high-quality gaming experiences for players around the world. We specialize in developing engaging mobile and PC games across multiple genres, including simulation, action, racing, puzzle, casual, and adventure. Our mission is to combine creativity, innovation, and polished gameplay to deliver entertaining experiences that players can enjoy anytime, anywhere. We focus on intuitive controls, attractive visuals, optimized performance, and regular updates based on player feedback. At Paradox EXE Games, we are passionate about building unique games that inspire excitement, challenge, and long-lasting enjoyment. Whether you're looking for fast-paced action, relaxing casual gameplay, or strategic adventures, our goal is to create games that keep players coming back for more..

Every project starts as a two-line "what if" and gets built out from there, in short cycles, in public where possible, with a soft spot for jank that has personality over polish that doesn't.

// HOW WE BUILD

  • ENGINE Unity (C#)
  • TEAM Solo dev + collaborators
  • STATUS Actively shipping prototypes

Most builds start life as a rough Unity prototype: one mechanic, tested until it's either fun or obviously not. The ones that survive get art, sound, and a real name. The ones that don't get cannibalized for parts in the next one.
